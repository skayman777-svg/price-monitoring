Online Store Price Monitoring

The script crawls the catalog section by section and page by page, saves a snapshot of prices and stock, compares it with the previous one and builds an Excel report plus a Telegram summary. Below is the output of a real run against the TechnoHome test store: two catalog snapshots and their comparison.
